## 2. Codebase Architecture & File Exploration

### Project Structure Analysis

- **`astro.config.mjs`**: Configured with `output: 'static'`, `site: 'https://airesacondicionadoscancun.vercel.app'`, `@astrojs/sitemap` integration, and `@astrojs/vercel` adapter with web analytics enabled.
- **`public/robots.txt`**: Standard crawler permissions (`Allow: /`) referencing `https://airesacondicionadoscancun.vercel.app/sitemap-index.xml`.
- **`src/pages/[...slug].astro`**: Single dynamic catch-all route driving all 50 content pages via Astro Content Collections (`getCollection('pages')`).
- **`src/content.config.ts`**: Zod schema definition for content pages (`title`, `description`, `h1`, `type` [`hub`, `spoke`, `legal`], `service`, `locationName`, `locationSlug`, `parentHub`, `landmarks`, `neighborhoodContext`).
- **`src/content/pages/`**: 50 Markdown content pages:
  - `index.md`: Homepage / Core Hub.
  - 4 Service Hub pages (`mantenimiento`, `reparacion`, `instalacion`, `carga-de-gas`).
  - 40 Location Spoke pages (10 geographic zones × 4 service types).
  - 5 Operational & Legal pages (`contacto`, `cotizacion-estimaciones`, `nosotros`, `privacidad`, `terminos`).
- **`src/layouts/Layout.astro`**: Central layout shell containing:
  - Canonical URL generation via `Astro.url.pathname`.
  - Google Tag Manager & Google Consent Mode v2 scripts.
  - Open Graph (`og:type`, `og:url`, `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:locale`, `og:site_name`) and Twitter Card metadata.
  - JSON-LD Structured Data: `HVACBusiness` and `BreadcrumbList`.
  - Font optimization (`Outfit` and `Inter` via Google Fonts preconnect).
  - Mobile-first CSS styling and responsive layout grid.
- **`src/components/`**:
  - `FAQSection.astro`: Accordion FAQ with direct technical answers.
  - `LeadForm.astro`: High-converting lead form with floating WhatsApp contact integration.
  - `SiloLinks.astro`: Automated internal SILO navigation links based on page position.
  - `CookieBanner.astro`: GDPR/CCPA consent banner integration.
- **`src/utils/siloLinking.ts` & `src/utils/siloLinking.test.ts`**: Pure algorithm for internal SILO interlinking with 100% test coverage validating zero orphan pages across all 50 URLs.

## 3. Detailed Audit by SEO Dimension

### A. Technical SEO (92/100)
- **Indexability & Routing**: 50 static HTML pages cleanly generated by Astro SSG. No orphan pages.
- **Sitemap & Robots**: `@astrojs/sitemap` generates `sitemap-index.xml` automatically. `public/robots.txt` points directly to the sitemap index.
- **Security Headers**: Configured in `vercel.json` with `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, and `Strict-Transport-Security`.
- **Canonical URLs**: Present on 100% of pages with consistent domain mapping.
- **Minor Gap**: `sitemap-index.xml` route exists in Astro build output, but ensuring XML sitemap priority tags for Hubs vs. Spokes could further optimize crawl budget.

### B. Content Quality & E-E-A-T (88/100)
- **Local Contextualization**: Highly contextualized technical vocabulary addressing Cancún's tropical climate challenges (saline sea breeze, 85%+ relative humidity, CFE voltage spikes, R410A/R32 refrigerant specs).
- **Content Volume**: 900+ words on homepage, 2,000+ words per Spoke and Hub page.
- **Trust Signals**: Written 90–365 day warranty, <90 minute response time guarantee, 24/7 service availability.
- **Readability**: Flesch-Kincaid analysis shows technical jargon ("condensadores", "evaporadores", "microfaradios") elevates complexity score. Some paragraph blocks exceed 35 words.

### C. On-Page SEO (95/100)
- **Title Tags**: Optimally structured (`<Service> en <Location> | Aires Acondicionados Cancún 24/7`), under 60 characters.
- **Meta Descriptions**: Compelling descriptions under 160 characters with explicit CTAs.
- **Heading Hierarchy**: Strict single `<h1>` per page, followed by logical `<h2>` and `<h3>` tags.
- **Social Tags**: Open Graph and Twitter Card tags present.
- **Critical Gap**: Missing `<meta property="og:image">` and `<meta name="twitter:image">` tags in `Layout.astro` `<head>`.

### D. Schema / Structured Data (98/100)
- **JSON-LD Implementation**: `HVACBusiness` schema includes correct geo-coordinates (21.1619, -86.8515), exact Cancún address, telephone, 24/7 opening hours, and service areas.
- **Breadcrumb Schema**: `BreadcrumbList` schema reflects the exact SILO hierarchy.
- **Google Policy Compliance**: 0 commercial `FAQPage` JSON-LD blocks (complying with Google's August 2023 restrictions on commercial FAQ rich results).
- **Opportunity**: Add itemized `Service` schema JSON-LD on Spoke pages to enhance Google Knowledge Graph understanding of specific HVAC offerings per neighborhood.

### E. Performance & Core Web Vitals (90/100)
- **Zero-JS Overhead**: Static HTML rendering with minimal client-side JS (<1 KB).
- **Resource Hints**: Preconnect configured for Google Fonts.
- **Estimated CWV**: LCP < 1.2s, INP < 50ms, CLS = 0.0.

### F. Image Optimization & Visual E-E-A-T (75/100)
- **Current State**: Web pages currently use CSS graphics, SVG icons, and color gradients without actual content `<img>` tags.
- **Gap**: Missing physical photography of HVAC technicians, tools, and real Cancún locations.
- **Action Required**: Add optimized WebP images with descriptive geolocalized `alt` text, explicit `width` and `height` attributes (preventing CLS), and an `og-image.jpg` asset for social previews.

### G. AI Search Readiness (GEO / AEO) (96/100)
- **Direct Answer Blocks**: 40–60 word summary answers formatted for AI Answer Engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google Search Overviews).
- **Semantic Accordion**: Accessible `<details>` / `<summary>` tags in `FAQSection.astro`.

### H. Local SEO & GBP Alignment
- **Google Business Profile Alignment**: Detailed in `GBP-GUIDE.md`, mapping NAP data, lat/long coordinates, primary/secondary categories (`Air Conditioning Repair Service`, `Air Conditioning Contractor`), and service areas to match website schema.

## 6. Risks & Mitigation Strategies

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
